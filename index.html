Privacy Policy - Store Boss: Supermarket Tycoon

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Store Boss: Supermarket Tycoon ("the game") is published by Umair ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email address or any account details. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some data as described below.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad during play, an occasional full-screen ad between game days, and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location (country or region, derived from the IP address),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps), and diagnostic data such as crash information from the ad software.

This data is collected directly by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time you open the game you are asked whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with AD PRIVACY in the game's pause menu.
  • US states: where state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data in the same consent screen.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Rewarded video ads are always optional: the game never requires you to watch one to keep playing.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stores, money, staff, settings such as volume and game speed) and the daily ad-reward counters are saved locally on your device using the platform's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. You can erase your progress at any time with RESET SAVE in the game's settings.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to load ads and the advertising ID permission used by the ad service. It does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, files or other apps.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Data deletion

We do not hold any of your data on our side. Uninstalling the game deletes everything the game stored on your device. For data held by Google, see the Google links above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
